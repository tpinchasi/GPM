# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to build something good, as you are.

## What we expect

- Treat people with respect, including when you think they are wrong.
- Critique the code, the design or the decision — not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that a well-reasoned "no" is a legitimate answer, and give your reasons when you say it.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, and move on without ceremony.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing
others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussion. Sexualised language or
imagery has no place here.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately, through the contact in the repository's profile. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not uphold this in good faith may be removed from
the project.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
